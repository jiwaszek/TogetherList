TogetherList is the shopping list you share with the people you live with. Add something on your phone and it appears on theirs straight away. When you're done shopping, TogetherList keeps track of who paid and who owes whom.

SHOP TOGETHER
• Shared lists that sync in real time between everyone on the list
• Works offline in the shop, and syncs when you're back online
• Quick entry: type “2 kg apples” and the quantity is filled in for you
• Products are grouped by aisle automatically, in English and Polish
• Private lists stay private: you choose exactly which lists to share

INVITE IN SECONDS
• Show a QR code, send a link or use AirDrop
• Standing next to each other? Just bring your iPhones together
• Once you're connected, share more lists with one tap, no new code needed

SPLIT THE BILL
• Check out after shopping: pick what you bought, enter the total and who paid
• Costs are split equally, down to the cent
• Balances show who owes whom. Record a payment once the money is sent
• History keeps every shop, searchable by product, shop or person
• Choose your currency: Polish złoty, euro, dollar and many more

YOU'RE IN CONTROL
• Invitations expire after 48 hours and can be cancelled any time
• Leave a list, remove someone or disconnect whenever you like
• Delete your account and your data from inside the app

TogetherList is available in English and Polish.
